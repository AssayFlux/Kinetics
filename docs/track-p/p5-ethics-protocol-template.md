# P5 — Ethics / adviser review protocol template

**TEMPLATE ONLY — NOT A STUDY PROTOCOL, ETHICS APPROVAL OR RECRUITMENT AUTHORIZATION.** No recruitment, consent collection, identifiable/clinical record access or prospective evaluation may begin without competent ethics determination and all required approvals.

## Administrative
Protocol title/version/date; principal investigator and qualifications; sponsor/institution; sites/jurisdictions; funding/conflicts; clinical PGx adviser; privacy/security lead.

## Research question and rationale
Specific question; source/version background; necessity of human participants/data; synthetic/non-human alternatives; primary/secondary objectives.

## Design and participants
Design/setting; target population; inclusion/exclusion; recruitment method; sample-size and power rationale; vulnerable-population safeguards; compensation.

## Data and privacy
Data categories/provenance; identifiability; lawful authority/consent; minimisation/access roles; storage/encryption/key lifecycle; retention/deletion/withdrawal; cross-border transfer; breach response.

## Risks and safety
Physical, psychological, informational and social risks; minimisation; potential benefits without overstatement; safety monitoring; adverse-event escalation; stop/pause criteria; incidental findings and return-of-results policy.

## Analysis and reporting
Pre-specified endpoints; statistical plan; missing-data/subgroup plan; bias/ancestry coverage; independent monitoring/adjudication; publication/negative-results policy.

## Ethics submission
Competent ethics body; determination requested; submission reference/date; approval/determination document; conditions/expiry; consent and participant-information versions; institutional/legal/privacy/security approvals.

## Sign-off
Record actual reviewer identity, role, competence, organization, date, findings, conditions and evidence. Do not represent placeholders as approvals.
