# Brand usage — owner confirmation required

The single canonical asset root is `assets/`. The owner-supplied organization master is `assets/brand/brand-logo.svg`; its Git blob is byte-identical to the former `Assets/Brand Logo.svg`. The supplied animated files are preserved at `assets/brand/organization-logo.gif` and `assets/brand/kinetics-logo.gif`.

Not yet specified: official brand colour hex values, minimum reproduction size, clear-space measurement, approved backgrounds, permitted/forbidden variants, and usage/clearance terms. Do not infer these from the artwork. Do not recolour, redraw, crop, stretch, simplify, add effects, or create dark/light variants without explicit owner approval.

The organization logo and Kinetics app mark are distinct assets. The presence of an animated logo does not automatically approve it as the Android adaptive launcher icon. The app-icon placeholders remain development-only until the owner-approved app mark and all release usage requirements are supplied.
