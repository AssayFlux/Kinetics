# Clinical translation and safety

This document describes a future evidence pathway. It does not claim Kinetics is a medical device or clinically validated.

## Research-to-care boundary
Kinetics begins as research software. A prediction, biomarker, simulation or candidate ranking is not a treatment recommendation. No autonomous diagnosis, prescription, dose adjustment or medication change is permitted in the research platform.

## Evidence ladder
1. Computational plausibility and reproducible methods.
2. Analytical validity for the defined computation/measurement.
3. Independent scientific validity across datasets, laboratories or cohorts.
4. Clinical validity in the intended population and context.
5. Clinical utility compared with current standard care, including harms.
6. Regulatory and operational readiness for the exact intended purpose and jurisdiction.

Passing one level does not imply the next.

## Future requirements
- Qualified clinical, statistical, bioinformatics and regulatory collaborators.
- Ethics review and informed consent or another lawful basis.
- Prospective protocols and preregistered analysis plans where appropriate.
- Representative cohorts and external validation.
- Appropriate discrimination, calibration, decision-curve and subgroup metrics.
- Assessment of false positives/negatives and downstream harms.
- Human-factors and workflow evaluation.
- Clear communication of uncertainty and limitations.
- Drift, bias, security and performance monitoring.
- Quality management, risk management, cybersecurity and jurisdiction-specific regulatory review.

## Privacy
Genetic and health information are highly sensitive. Apply data minimization, purpose limitation, consent/legal basis, role-based access, encryption, auditability, retention and verified deletion. De-identification does not guarantee anonymity. Never commit personal health data, identifiable genomic data, credentials or restricted datasets to this public repository.

## Treatment-response research
Drug response is multifactorial. A genotype or model score alone rarely determines outcome. Research must consider phenotype, disease severity, organ function, co-medications, interactions, environment, population applicability, evidence quality and patient preferences where relevant and lawfully available. Authoritative pharmacogenomic evidence must retain its source, version, strength and context.

Clinical deployment is a separate gated programme. No research milestone automatically authorizes patient-care use.
